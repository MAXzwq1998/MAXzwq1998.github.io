Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 June 2026

Saint Novel ("Saint Novel," "we," "us," or "our") respects the privacy of individuals who visit our website, contact us, submit partnering or platform-access inquiries, apply for opportunities, or otherwise interact with us.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and protect personal data in connection with our website and related business interactions. It is intended to provide clear notice of our privacy practices for website visitors, prospective partners, collaborators, candidates, vendors, advisers, and other individuals who communicate with Saint Novel.

For the purposes of this Privacy Policy, "personal data" means information that identifies, relates to, describes, or can reasonably be associated with an identifiable individual, subject to applicable law.

For purposes of Hong Kong personal data protection law, Saint Novel is the data user responsible for the personal data described in this Privacy Policy. Where other privacy laws apply, Saint Novel may be treated as a controller, organization, business, or similar responsible party.

1. Scope of this Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to personal data that Saint Novel collects through or in connection with:

  • our website;
  • contact, inquiry, partnering, or platform-access forms;
  • recruitment, talent-interest, or career-related forms;
  • email correspondence and other direct communications with us;
  • business-development, partnering, vendor, scientific, or professional interactions; and
  • ordinary website analytics, security, and operational activities.

This Privacy Policy is intended to provide general notice of Saint Novel's personal data practices. Where specific privacy laws apply, individuals may have additional rights under those laws.

This Privacy Policy does not govern confidential technical, scientific, research, clinical, commercial, or business information submitted to Saint Novel under a separate non-disclosure agreement, collaboration agreement, research agreement, data processing agreement, service agreement, informed-consent document, or other written contract. Where a separate written agreement applies, that agreement will govern the relevant information and relationship.

This Privacy Policy also does not apply to clinical-trial data, patient-facing services, research-subject data, human-subject data, patient-identifiable data, or clinical-care data unless Saint Novel expressly states otherwise in a separate privacy notice or written agreement.

Unless Saint Novel has expressly requested such information under an appropriate written agreement, please do not submit patient-identifiable information, human-subject data, genetic or genomic data relating to identifiable individuals, clinical data, confidential partner program information, unpublished experimental data, or other sensitive scientific information through general website forms.
